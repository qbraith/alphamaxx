**Core PCE** — Personal Consumption Expenditures inflation excluding food and
energy. This is **the specific number the Federal Reserve targets at 2%**, making
it the most policy-relevant inflation reading of all.

**Importance: 🔴 Tier 1.** When the Fed talks about "getting inflation back to
target," this is the series they mean.

**How it moves markets:**
- **Above expectations:** distance from the 2% goal widens → hawkish → higher
  yields, firmer USD, weaker rate-sensitive equities.
- **Below expectations:** progress toward target → dovish → lower yields, equity
  support.

Because CPI (released ~2 weeks earlier) lets analysts estimate Core PCE in
advance, surprises are usually smaller — but a deviation from the expected path
still repositions rate-cut odds.
