**Core CPI** — the Consumer Price Index excluding food and energy, whose prices
are volatile. This is the number professionals watch most closely because it
better reflects the *underlying* inflation trend the Fed is trying to control.

**Importance: 🔴 Tier 1.** The month-over-month Core CPI surprise is often the
single most important data point in the entire CPI release.

**How it moves markets:**
- **Above forecast:** signals sticky inflation → hawkish for the Fed → higher
  yields, stronger USD, pressure on equities (rate-sensitive tech hit hardest).
- **Below forecast:** disinflation narrative strengthens → lower yields, weaker
  USD, relief rally in stocks.

A 0.1% miss or beat on the monthly core figure can swing the S&P 500 by more than
1% within minutes. Compare the print to both the consensus forecast and the prior
month's pace.
