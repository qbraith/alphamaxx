**Nonfarm Payrolls (NFP) / The Jobs Report** — the monthly US employment report
showing how many jobs the economy added, plus the unemployment rate and average
hourly earnings. Released the first Friday of each month at 8:30 AM ET.

**Importance: 🔴 Tier 1.** This is the highest-profile economic release of the
month and routinely the largest single-day macro catalyst for stocks, bonds, and
the dollar.

**How it moves markets:**
- **Stronger than expected (more jobs / hot wages):** signals a resilient economy
  but also inflation risk → can push the Fed to stay restrictive → yields up, USD
  up; stocks reaction is two-sided (good growth vs. higher-for-longer rates).
- **Weaker than expected:** raises rate-cut odds but also recession worry → yields
  down, USD down; equities often rally on cut hopes unless the miss is severe
  enough to spark growth fear ("bad news is bad news" regime).
- **Average Hourly Earnings** is the inflation angle within the report — hot wage
  growth is hawkish regardless of the headline jobs number.

Watch the headline payrolls vs. consensus, the unemployment rate, *and* wage
growth together — they can tell conflicting stories.
