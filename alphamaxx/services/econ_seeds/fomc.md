**FOMC Rate Decision** — the Federal Open Market Committee announces its target
range for the federal funds rate, the anchor for all US interest rates. Eight
scheduled meetings a year, each with a statement at 2:00 PM ET followed by the
Chair's press conference at 2:30 PM ET.

**Importance: 🔴 Tier 1 (the biggest of all).** Nothing moves global markets more
than the Fed. The decision, the statement wording, the updated projections (the
"dot plot," at quarterly meetings), and the press conference tone each move
markets in turn.

**How it moves markets:**
- **Hawkish surprise (higher rates / fewer cuts signaled):** yields up, USD up,
  equities down — especially growth/tech and small caps.
- **Dovish surprise (cuts signaled / softer tone):** yields down, USD down,
  equities rally, gold up.
- The *decision itself* is often priced in; the **forward guidance** and the
  Chair's press conference usually drive the bigger intraday swings.

Key things to parse: the dot plot (rate path), changes in the statement language,
and how the Chair characterizes inflation and the labor market in the Q&A.
