**Housing Data (Starts, Permits, New & Existing Home Sales)** — measures of
activity in the US housing market, one of the most interest-rate-sensitive parts
of the economy.

**Importance: 🟡 Tier 3.** Individually secondary movers, but valuable as an early
read on how Fed policy is transmitting to the real economy, since housing reacts
quickly to mortgage rates.

**How it moves markets:**
- **Stronger housing:** economic resilience despite high rates → mildly risk-on,
  can be hawkish.
- **Weaker housing:** rate-sensitivity biting → signals policy is working, growth
  cooling.

Building permits lead starts, which lead completions, so permits are the most
forward-looking of the group. Reaction is biggest when rates and the housing
cycle are central market themes.
