**Retail Sales** — measures the total receipts of US retail stores, the best
monthly read on consumer spending, which drives roughly two-thirds of the economy.

**Importance: 🟠 Tier 2.** A meaningful mover because the consumer is the engine of
US growth. The "control group" figure (which feeds GDP) matters more than the
headline.

**How it moves markets:**
- **Stronger:** resilient consumer → supports growth and earnings, but can be
  hawkish (Fed can stay tight) → yields up, stocks two-sided.
- **Weaker:** demand cooling → recession worry but also rate-cut hopes → yields
  down.

Strip out autos and gas (volatile) and focus on the control group for the cleanest
signal of underlying consumption strength.
