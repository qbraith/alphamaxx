**Durable Goods Orders** — new orders for long-lasting manufactured goods
(vehicles, machinery, aircraft). A gauge of business investment and manufacturing
demand.

**Importance: 🟡 Tier 3.** Usually a minor mover and quite volatile, since a few
large aircraft or defense orders can swing the headline.

**How it moves markets:**
- **Stronger:** healthy business investment → mildly risk-positive.
- **Weaker:** soft capital spending → growth concern.

Focus on **core capital goods orders (non-defense, ex-aircraft)** — the cleaner
proxy for underlying business investment that feeds GDP. The headline alone is
often misleading.
