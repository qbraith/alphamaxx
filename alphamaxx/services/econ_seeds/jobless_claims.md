**Initial Jobless Claims** — the number of people filing for unemployment
benefits for the first time, released **weekly** (Thursdays, 8:30 AM ET). The most
timely read on the labor market.

**Importance: 🟡 Tier 2–3.** Usually a minor mover, but its high frequency makes
it the fastest signal of a turning labor market, and it can matter a lot near
turning points or when markets are laser-focused on employment.

**How it moves markets:**
- **Rising claims:** labor market softening → dovish (cut odds up), but a steady
  climb feeds recession worry.
- **Falling/low claims:** labor market resilient → can be hawkish.

Watch the 4-week moving average to cut through weekly noise; single weeks are
volatile and often distorted by holidays or one-off factors.
