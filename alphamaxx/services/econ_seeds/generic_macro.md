**US Economic Release** — a scheduled US macroeconomic data point.

**Importance: depends on the indicator.** Market reaction is driven less by the
absolute number than by the **surprise versus the consensus forecast**, and by how
relevant the indicator is to the market's current focus (inflation, jobs, or
growth).

**General framework for how data moves markets:**
- **Hot growth / inflation data:** can be hawkish for the Fed → yields up, USD up,
  rate-sensitive equities pressured.
- **Soft data:** dovish (rate-cut hopes) but, if weak enough, sparks growth fear.
- The 2-year Treasury yield and Fed-funds futures are the cleanest gauges of how
  the market is repricing rate expectations after any release.
