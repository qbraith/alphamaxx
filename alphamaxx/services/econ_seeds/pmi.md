**ISM / PMI Surveys (Manufacturing & Services)** — diffusion indexes from surveys
of purchasing managers. A reading **above 50 means expansion, below 50 means
contraction**. The ISM Services index is especially important since services
dominate the US economy.

**Importance: 🟠 Tier 2 (Services can be Tier 1).** Timely, forward-looking
sentiment data released early in the month — often the first read on the prior
month's economy.

**How it moves markets:**
- **Above 50 / rising:** economic expansion → risk-on, supportive of equities; a
  hot prices-paid sub-index is the inflation warning to watch.
- **Below 50 / falling:** contraction signal → growth fear → equities down, yields
  down on cut hopes.

The **prices-paid** and **employment** sub-indexes inside the report often move
markets as much as the headline, since they preview inflation and the jobs report.
