**PCE (Personal Consumption Expenditures Price Index)** — a broad measure of US
consumer inflation released by the BEA. Crucially, **PCE is the Fed's preferred
inflation gauge** for its 2% target.

**Importance: 🟠 Tier 1–2.** Because the Fed targets PCE, it carries policy
weight, though it often moves markets less than CPI because CPI is released
earlier and largely telegraphs the PCE result.

**How it moves markets:**
- **Hotter:** reinforces a restrictive Fed → yields up, USD up, equities pressured.
- **Cooler:** supports rate cuts → yields down, equities up.

The release also includes personal income and spending data, which give a read on
consumer health. Focus on the Core PCE month-over-month and year-over-year figures.
