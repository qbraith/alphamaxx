**PPI (Producer Price Index)** — measures the prices producers receive for goods
and services, i.e. inflation at the wholesale level *before* it reaches consumers.

**Importance: 🟠 Tier 2.** A solid market mover, partly as a leading indicator of
consumer inflation and partly because several PPI components feed directly into
the Fed's preferred PCE inflation gauge.

**How it moves markets:**
- **Hotter:** signals pipeline inflation pressure → hawkish lean → yields up.
- **Cooler:** disinflation in the pipeline → supportive of risk assets.

PPI is often released near CPI; analysts use the two together to refine their PCE
estimates. Reaction is usually smaller than CPI unless the surprise is large.
