**Corporate Earnings Release** — a company reports quarterly results: revenue,
earnings per share (EPS), margins, and (often most importantly) **forward
guidance**.

**Importance: 🔴 high for mega-caps.** For the largest companies (the kind on a
watchlist), earnings can move not just the stock but entire indexes and sectors,
since a handful of mega-caps drive a large share of the S&P 500 and Nasdaq.

**How it moves markets:**
- **Beat on EPS/revenue + strong guidance:** stock typically rallies; sector peers
  and index futures often follow.
- **Miss or weak guidance:** stock sells off, can drag the broader market and
  related names.
- **Guidance usually matters more than the reported quarter** — markets are
  forward-looking. A beat with cautious guidance can still fall.

Watch the after-hours reaction (most report before the open or after the close),
the magnitude of the move versus the options-implied expected move, and any read-
through to suppliers, competitors, and the company's end markets.
