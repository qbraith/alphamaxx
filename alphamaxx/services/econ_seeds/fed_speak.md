**Fed Speakers / Minutes / Testimony** — public remarks by Federal Reserve
officials (especially the Chair), Congressional testimony, and the minutes of
past FOMC meetings.

**Importance: 🟠 Tier 2 (can spike to Tier 1 for the Chair).** Individual
speeches usually cause modest moves, but remarks from the Chair, or any hint that
shifts the expected rate path, can move markets sharply.

**How it moves markets:**
- **Hawkish comments** (inflation not beaten, no rush to cut): yields up, USD up,
  equities soften.
- **Dovish comments** (progress on inflation, openness to cuts): yields down,
  equities firm.
- **FOMC minutes** can reveal how united or divided the committee is, reshaping
  expectations even weeks after the meeting.

Voting members and the Chair carry far more weight than non-voters. Reaction is
biggest when a speaker deviates from the market's current assumption.
