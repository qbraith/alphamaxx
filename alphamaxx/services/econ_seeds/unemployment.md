**Unemployment Rate** — the share of the labor force without a job but actively
seeking one. Released as part of the monthly jobs report (see NFP).

**Importance: 🔴 Tier 1 (as part of the jobs report).** A key gauge of labor
market health and an input the Fed weighs against its inflation mandate. It also
underpins recession signals like the Sahm Rule.

**How it moves markets:**
- **Rising unemployment:** softer labor market → raises rate-cut odds, but a sharp
  jump stokes recession fear → yields down; equities can rally (cut hopes) or fall
  (growth scare) depending on the regime.
- **Falling unemployment:** labor-market strength → can be hawkish if it implies
  wage/inflation pressure.

Read it together with payrolls and wage growth; a single tenth can shift the
narrative when the market is focused on labor-market cooling.
