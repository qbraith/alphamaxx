**JOLTS (Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey)** — measures job openings, hires,
and quits. Job openings indicate labor demand; the quits rate signals worker
confidence and future wage pressure.

**Importance: 🟡 Tier 2.** The Fed has repeatedly cited the openings-to-unemployed
ratio as a gauge of labor-market tightness, which gives JOLTS more weight than its
lag (it's a month behind) would otherwise suggest.

**How it moves markets:**
- **More openings / high quits:** tight labor market, wage-inflation risk →
  hawkish lean → yields up.
- **Fewer openings / falling quits:** cooling demand → dovish, supports rate-cut
  expectations.

A clean rebalancing (openings falling without a jump in unemployment) is the
"soft landing" signal markets like to see.
